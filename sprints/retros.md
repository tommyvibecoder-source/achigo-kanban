# Sprint Retrospective

## Sprint 1: MVP Delivery (2026-08-20)

### What Went Well
- Seamless integration of Jira workflow paradigms (Kanban board, backlog, issue keys) and Confluence documentation paradigms (living PRD, natural language problem statements).
- Clear consensus gate mechanism guaranteeing that no idea enters production without unanimous team agreement.
- 1-click AI Prompt generator bridging human product management directly with autonomous AI developers (Antigravity, Claude, Openwork).
- Rapid test coverage on core scoring and governance logic.

### Areas for Future Optimization
- Add real-time multi-device WebSockets sync for distributed founders.
- Expand AI prompt templates to support mobile-specific SwiftUI / Jetpack Compose and backend microservice targets.
